**ChatGPT**, **Claude**, and **Google Gemini** are the three assistants most people actually open every day. They all chat, draft, and summarize. They do **not** feel the same once you use them for real work — long documents, code, or research with sources.

This is a practical comparison, not a ranking contest. Plans and model names change; judge the product by the job you need done this month.

## ChatGPT — the default generalist

Choose ChatGPT when you want **one place** for brainstorming, drafts, light coding, and (on paid tiers) image generation. The ecosystem is the widest: tutorials, GPTs, and mobile apps are everywhere.

Watch-outs: output can sound generic unless you give a tight brief. For job applications, always edit — then run a parse check in our [ATS Resume Checker](/ai-tools/ats-resume-checker) or draft a tailored letter with the [AI Cover Letter Generator](/ai-tools/cover-letter-generator).

Try it: <a href="https://chatgpt.com/" rel="sponsored nofollow">Open ChatGPT</a>

## Claude — long context and careful tone

Claude often feels stronger on **long PDFs**, multi-file coding, and prose that should not sound like a press release. If your work is policies, research notes, or a big codebase, start here.

Watch-outs: fewer “one app does images + voice + plugins” extras than ChatGPT. If you only need a 3-sentence email, any of the three is fine.

Try it: <a href="https://claude.ai/" rel="sponsored nofollow">Open Claude</a>

## Gemini — when your files already live in Google

Gemini is the natural pick if you already live in **Gmail, Docs, Drive, and Search**. Grounding in Google’s index helps current-events questions. Workspace users save the most time here.

Watch-outs: quality still varies by prompt and region. Do not paste secrets into any cloud assistant — including Gemini.

Try it: <a href="https://gemini.google.com/" rel="sponsored nofollow">Open Gemini</a>

## Which one should you pay for?

Pay only after the **free** cap blocks real work (daily limits, slower models, or missing features you use weekly).

| You mostly… | Start with |
|-------------|------------|
| Mixed tasks, images, plugins | ChatGPT paid |
| Long docs / careful code review | Claude paid |
| Docs + Gmail + Search all day | Gemini / Google AI paid |
| Occasional drafts | Stay on a free tier |

Before you buy: dump a real brief into all three free chats. Keep the one that needs the least cleanup.

**ChatGPT** is the flexible default. **Claude** is the long-document and coding specialist. **Gemini** is the Google Workspace specialist. Pick the one that matches where your files already live — then pay only if the free plan is the bottleneck.
