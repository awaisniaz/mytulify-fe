Flooring, seed, gravel, and paint all start with area. A **square footage calculator** should match the shape, because forcing a curve into length × width either wastes material or leaves a gap.

- Rectangle: length × width
- Triangle: (base × height) ÷ 2
- Circle: π × radius²
- Trapezoid: ((base A + base B) ÷ 2) × height

The [Square Footage Calculator](/calculators/square-footage-calculator) reports square feet and square meters (1 sq ft ≈ 0.0929 sq m). Split an L-shaped room into two rectangles and add the results. Do not include closets you are not covering, and do not forget them if you are.

From there, gallons come from the [Paint Calculator](/calculators/paint-calculator), stone from the [Gravel Calculator](/calculators/gravel-calculator), and a slab from the [Concrete Calculator](/calculators/concrete-calculator). Area is the shared input. Thickness and coverage are not.
