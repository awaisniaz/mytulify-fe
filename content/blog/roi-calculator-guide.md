An **ROI calculator** helps you answer a simple question: *Relative to what I put in, how much did I get back?* **ROI** (return on investment) expresses that answer as a percentage of cost, so you can compare campaigns, projects, products, or portfolio moves on the same scale.

This is a **planning** view. Markets, fees, taxes, and accounting rules can change the “true” economic result. Always treat calculator output as a scenario, not advice.

## What an ROI calculator needs

| Input | Why it matters |
|-------|----------------|
| Cost / initial investment | The amount you spent or invested |
| Net gain *or* final value | Profit/loss, or what the investment is worth now |
| Holding period (years, optional) | Enables annualized ROI and simple annual ROI |
| Target ROI % (optional reverse) | Finds the gain and final value needed to hit a goal |

Open the [ROI Calculator](/calculators/roi-calculator) and pick **cost + net gain** or **cost + final value**, depending on the numbers you already have.

## How ROI is calculated (plain language)

Mytulify’s tool uses the standard planning formulas:

1. If you enter **net gain**, final value = cost + gain.
2. If you enter **final value**, net gain = final value − cost.
3. **ROI %** = `(net gain ÷ cost) × 100`.
4. **Return multiple** = `final value ÷ cost` (for example, 1.25× means you have 125% of your cost).
5. With a positive holding period and positive final value, **annualized ROI** = `((final ÷ cost)^(1 ÷ years) − 1) × 100` — the same compound idea as CAGR.
6. **Simple annual ROI** = total ROI % ÷ years (no compounding).

Negative gain produces a negative ROI (a loss). Annualized ROI is not defined when final value is zero or negative, because compound growth from a non-positive ending value is not a meaningful percentage path.

### Worked planning example

Suppose:

- Cost: 10,000
- Net gain: 2,500
- Holding period: 2 years

Then final value is 12,500, ROI is **25%**, and the return multiple is **1.25×**. Annualized ROI over two years is about **11.80%**, while simple annual ROI is **12.5% / yr**. Plug the same numbers into the [ROI Calculator](/calculators/roi-calculator) to see every field update instantly in your browser.

## Why annualized ROI matters

A single ROI % answers “how much relative to cost?” It does **not** answer “how fast?” A 25% gain in six months is very different from 25% over five years. Use the holding-period fields when you compare investments or projects that ran for different lengths of time.
