**CAGR** (Compound Annual Growth Rate) is the single yearly growth rate that takes a starting value to an ending value over a set number of years — as if growth were perfectly smooth. Analysts use a **CAGR calculator** to compare mutual funds, stocks, revenue, and other metrics on an equal footing when year-to-year returns bounce around.

## Why CAGR beats “total return %” for multi-year periods

Suppose an investment doubles in five years. Total gain is 100%, but that is not “100% per year.” CAGR answers: *what constant annual rate would produce that same ending value?* That number is the fair way to compare a 3-year fund with a 7-year fund, or revenue in 2020 vs 2026.

CAGR does **not** describe the path — only the endpoints. Volatility, interim deposits, and withdrawals are outside the classic formula.

## The CAGR formula

**CAGR = (Ending value ÷ Beginning value)^(1 ÷ years) − 1**

Multiply by 100 for a percentage.

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Beginning value | Value at the start of the period |
| Ending value | Value at the end of the period |
| Years | Length of the period (can be fractional, e.g. 3.5) |

### Worked example

- Begin: ₹1,00,000  
- End: ₹2,50,000  
- Years: 5  

CAGR = (2.5)^(1/5) − 1 ≈ **20.11%** per year

## When CAGR is the right tool

Use CAGR when you have a clear start value, end value, and time span:

- Fund NAV or portfolio value over N years (no mid-period cash flows, or after adjusting for them)  
- Company revenue or profit growth across fiscal years  
- Comparing two assets held for different lengths of time  

Avoid classic CAGR when:

- You add money every month (use a [SIP calculator](/calculators/sip-calculator) for projections)  
- You need risk-adjusted return (Sharpe, drawdowns)  
- The period includes large one-off deposits or withdrawals without adjustment  

## CAGR vs compound interest vs SIP

| Tool | Question it answers |
|------|---------------------|
| [CAGR Calculator](/calculators/cagr-calculator) | What annual rate links start → end over N years? |
| [Compound Interest Calculator](/calculators/compound-interest-calculator) | What will a starting amount (plus optional adds) grow to at a given rate? |
| [SIP Calculator](/calculators/sip-calculator) | What might regular monthly investments be worth at an assumed rate? |

CAGR looks **backward** (or across known endpoints). SIP and compound tools usually look **forward** under an assumed rate. For goal planning, project with SIP/compound; for performance review, summarize with CAGR.
