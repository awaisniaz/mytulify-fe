Quarries sell gravel by the ton. You measure a driveway in feet and inches. A **gravel calculator** is the translation between those units.

Cubic yards = length × width × (depth in inches ÷ 12) ÷ 27. Add about 10% for compaction and an edge that is never perfectly square. Tons = yards × tons per cubic yard. Crushed gravel is often near 1.4 tons per yard, sand near 1.3, and mulch much lighter, around 0.4. Using the gravel factor for mulch will over-order by a wide margin.

The [Gravel Calculator](/calculators/gravel-calculator) keeps that density editable and multiplies tons by your price per ton. Measure the area with the [Square Footage Calculator](/calculators/square-footage-calculator) when the shape is not a simple rectangle, then enter an equivalent length and width. If a concrete slab goes on top of the base, size the concrete separately with the [Concrete Calculator](/calculators/concrete-calculator). Depth is the compacted depth you want, not the fluffy height of a fresh pile.
