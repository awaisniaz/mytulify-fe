Handwritten notes are fast to capture and slow to reuse. You cannot search a notebook from your phone, and sharing a photo of scribbles rarely helps a teammate.

This guide shows how to turn one clear page into a usable summary with the free [Handwritten Notes Summarizer](/handwriting-ocr/handwritten-notes-summarizer).

## Pick the right output style

| Style | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| Bullet summary + action items | Meetings, standups, client calls |
| Structured outline | Long lectures with sections |
| Study flashcards | Definitions, formulas, exam prep |

If you only need a faithful transcript (no summary), use [Handwriting to Text (OCR)](/handwriting-ocr/handwriting-to-text) instead. For equations, use [Handwritten Math to LaTeX](/handwriting-ocr/handwritten-math-ocr).

## Page setup tips

- Capture **one page per upload** for accuracy.
- Prefer high-contrast ink; avoid torn edges in the frame.
- Add optional focus text when jargon matters — e.g. “keep drug names” or “this is a databases lecture.”

## Workflow

1. Photograph the page in good light.
2. Open the [Handwritten Notes Summarizer](/handwriting-ocr/handwritten-notes-summarizer).
3. Choose bullets, outline, or flashcards.
4. Generate, then skim for `[illegible]` markers and fill gaps from memory.
5. Paste into Notion, Docs, or Anki as needed.

Repeat for the next page and merge sections yourself — multi-page single shots usually hurt quality.

## Meeting notes vs study notes

**Meetings:** prioritize action items and owners. If the page has none, the summary should say so rather than invent tasks.

**Study:** flashcards should be short Q&A pairs. One dense page might yield 5–12 cards; split dense chapters across uploads.

## Privacy reminder

Photos are processed to produce the result and are not meant as a long-term archive. Do not upload confidential medical charts, passwords, or anything you would not send to another online service.

## Bottom line

Photograph clearly, summarize one page at a time, and treat AI output as a draft. You still own the facts — the tool just removes the retyping.
