A **fuel cost calculator** is gallons times price, and gallons are miles divided by MPG. That is the whole model. The useful part is comparing two cars on the same route and splitting the result.

Gallons = miles ÷ MPG. Cost = gallons × price per gallon. Cost per person = cost ÷ passengers. If you know liters per 100 km instead of MPG, convert with MPG ≈ 235.215 ÷ (L/100 km) before you enter the figure.

The [Fuel Cost Calculator](/calculators/fuel-cost-calculator) runs your MPG and a second MPG against one distance and one pump price, then shows the difference. Use a mixed MPG if the trip is not all highway. The sticker’s highway number will understate a city-heavy weekend. This does not include tolls, tires, or the loan on the car — payment math belongs in the [Loan Calculator](/calculators/loan-calculator).
