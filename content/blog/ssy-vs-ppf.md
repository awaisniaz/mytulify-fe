**SSY** (Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana) and **PPF** (Public Provident Fund) are both long-horizon, yearly-deposit style savings schemes that Indian families often compare side by side. They answer related questions — *If I put aside a fixed amount each year at a notified rate, what could maturity look like?* — but they are built for different eligibility stories and tenure shapes.

This article is educational planning content, not investment, tax, or legal advice. Scheme rules, rates, limits, and tax treatment change — verify with official sources, your bank or post office, and a qualified advisor when decisions involve real money.

## Snapshot comparison

| Factor | SSY | PPF |
|--------|-----|-----|
| Typical purpose | Girl-child education / marriage goal planning | General long-term / retirement-style savings |
| Deposit pattern often modeled | Yearly deposits for a limited window (commonly 15 years) | Yearly contributions across the tenure you model (often 15 years) |
| Tenure feel | Account life often longer than the deposit window (commonly illustrated as 21 years) | Standard block often 15 years, with possible extensions |
| Planning tool | [SSY Calculator](/calculators/ssy-calculator) | [PPF Calculator](/calculators/ppf-calculator) |
| Key modeling difference | Deposits stop; interest can continue | Contribute each year of the modeled tenure |

Use the table as a lens for *your* goal — not as a ranking of which scheme is “better.”

## When SSY is the natural starting point

If you are modeling a **girl-child account** with a **deposit window shorter than full maturity** — *If I deposit for 15 years and the account compounds until year 21, what corpus might I see?* — start with the [SSY Calculator](/calculators/ssy-calculator).

Practical steps:

1. Enter the yearly deposit you can sustain (within current scheme limits).
2. Enter the notified rate you want to assume.
3. Keep deposit years and maturity years separate (for example 15 and 21).
4. Optionally add an opening balance if the account already exists.

Deep dive on inputs and a worked example: [SSY Calculator Guide](/blog/ssy-calculator-guide).

## When PPF is the natural starting point

If you are modeling a **general PPF account** where you contribute each year of a standard block (and maybe extensions) — use the [PPF Calculator](/calculators/ppf-calculator). That tool answers a “contribute every year until the tenure ends” question; it does not split a shorter deposit window from a longer growth-only phase the way SSY planning often does.

For bank deposits without scheme-specific windows, compare with the [FD Calculator](/calculators/fd-calculator). For salary-linked retirement savings while employed, see the [EPF Calculator](/calculators/epf-calculator).

## How they fit together in a family plan

Many households use **both** views for different goals:

- **SSY calculator** ≈ girl-child corpus with deposits-then-growth
- **PPF calculator** ≈ household long-term savings with yearly contributions across the block

Running the same yearly amount through both tools is a useful stress test — but remember eligibility and purpose differ, so equal deposits do not mean interchangeable accounts.
