Typing integrals, matrices, and multi-line derivations by hand is slow. A clear photo plus OCR can get you usable LaTeX in seconds — if the image is set up well and you still verify the symbols.

This guide covers photo tips and how to use the free [Handwritten Math to LaTeX](/handwriting-ocr/handwritten-math-ocr) tool on Mytulify.

## When photo-to-LaTeX helps

- Moving homework or lecture notes into Overleaf or Markdown
- Capturing a whiteboard derivation after a meeting
- Building flashcards or docs without retyping every fraction

It is **not** a substitute for understanding the math. Always check exponents, limits, and matrix brackets before submitting graded work.

## Photo checklist for better OCR

1. **One equation (or one problem) per shot** — crop tightly.
2. **Even light, low glare** — avoid flash reflections on glossy paper.
3. **Dark ink on plain paper** — pencil on gray ruled paper is harder.
4. **Camera parallel to the page** — reduce perspective warp.
5. **Sharp focus** — if you cannot read it, the model will struggle too.

## Output modes

| Mode | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| Display LaTeX | You need a `latex` block for docs or Overleaf |
| Inline `$…$` | Embedding a short expression in a sentence |
| LaTeX + steps | The image is a problem you want solved or explained |

Use steps mode for practice and tutoring — then re-solve yourself to confirm.

## How to run a conversion

1. Photograph the equation.
2. Open [Handwritten Math to LaTeX](/handwriting-ocr/handwritten-math-ocr).
3. Upload the image and pick a format.
4. Copy the LaTeX into your editor and compile once to catch missing braces.

For plain prose notes (not equations), use [Handwriting to Text](/handwriting-ocr/handwriting-to-text) or the [Handwritten Notes Summarizer](/handwriting-ocr/handwritten-notes-summarizer).

## Common OCR mistakes to spot

- `1` vs `l` vs `|`
- Missing `\` on commands (`frac`, `int`, `sum`)
- Limits written next to the integral instead of `_` / `^`
- Matrix rows joined into one line

Fix those by hand — it is still faster than typing the whole expression.

## Bottom line

Good light and a tight crop matter more than the model. Convert, compile, and verify — then spend your time on the math, not the typing.
