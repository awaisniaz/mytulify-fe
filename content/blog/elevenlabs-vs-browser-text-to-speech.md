**ElevenLabs** sells cloud AI voices that sound close to a voice actor. **Browser text-to-speech** uses the voices already on your phone or laptop (no upload). Creators compare them because both can read a script — but one bills per character and the other is free and private.

If you are scripting YouTube, courses, or mockups, try on-device TTS first. Upgrade to ElevenLabs when the voice **is** the product (client ads, product narration, localization).

## ElevenLabs — when the voice has to sound hired

Pay for ElevenLabs when a stakeholder would notice a robotic OS voice: ads, trailer VO, multilingual product tours, or a consistent brand narrator.

Choose ElevenLabs when:

- You need a **named voice** across many videos  
- You must match a language the device does not speak well  
- Clients expect “studio” delivery, not Narrator.app  

Watch-outs: character caps, commercial-license details, and **never** clone a real person without rights.

Try it: <a href="https://elevenlabs.io/" rel="sponsored nofollow">ElevenLabs</a>

## Cost and privacy in one line

If the script cannot leave the laptop (unreleased course, internal comms), **do not** paste it into a cloud voice API. Use browser TTS. If the video is public marketing and quality is the bottleneck, ElevenLabs is the usual upgrade.
